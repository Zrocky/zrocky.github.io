Privacy Policy

Gridline (Gridline Ltd, registered in England and Wales) operates the Gridline app and gridline.co.uk. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect. Account details you give us (name, email), the listings you save, offers you send, and orders you place. Listing and order history is retained for accounting and consumer-rights compliance.

Payments. Card details are entered only on our hosted payment pages and are passed to our payment processor. Gridline does not store card numbers, expiry dates or security codes, and none of this ever touches your device storage.

What we never do. Sell your data, or share it with advertisers.

Data retention. Deleting your account in the app removes your profile, saved items, offers and order history from our active systems within 24 hours. Records required by UK tax law are kept in anonymised form.
